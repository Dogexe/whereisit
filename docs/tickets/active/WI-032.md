# WI-032 — Remove a bill from Home for the current cycle

Status: Draft

Spec: `docs/specs/bill-paid-toggle-on-home.md`

Codex profile: sol-high

Profile reason: the only release in this workstream touching the Supabase
schema and the sync mappers. A wrong mapper line does not fail loudly — it
silently drops a field on one device and not another, and last-write-wins sync
then propagates the loss.

Risk tier: High

## Goal

A bill can be swiped away from Home for the current cycle without being deleted
or marked paid, and it comes back on its own next cycle. The dismissal follows
the user across devices.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A new nullable text column `dismissed_cycle` exists on `public.bills`,
      added by a new timestamped migration under `supabase/migrations/`,
      following `20260828120000_bill_reminders_push.sql:48`'s
      `alter table public.bills add column if not exists <name> text;` shape.
- [ ] `rowToBill` (`sync.js:72`) and `billToRow` (`sync.js:73-79`) both map it,
      as `dismissedCycle` in app state and `dismissed_cycle` on the row,
      null-safe in both directions exactly like `last_paid_cycle` beside it.
- [ ] `upcomingBills()` excludes a bill whose `dismissedCycle` equals the
      current cycle, and stops excluding it once the cycle turns over. A
      dismissed bill returns on the 1st, like an overdue bill does.
- [ ] Dismissal is independent of paid state: a dismissed bill is hidden
      whether or not it is marked paid, and un-marking a hidden bill does not
      un-hide it.
- [ ] Home's bill row gains a second swipe circle, **Remove**, beside the
      Mark/Un-mark circle from `WI-031`. `actionCount` becomes 2 so the reveal
      geometry follows (`manage-row-swipe.js:17`).
- [ ] Remove does **not** take Delete's full-swipe-to-commit gesture and does
      not use the `manage-swipe-delete` class, which is what drives that
      behavior (`manage-row-swipe.js:33`, `:43-49`).
- [ ] Removing shows a toast with a working undo that restores the row, per
      `docs/UX.md`'s "Undo over confirm" — the same shape `deleteBill`
      (`settings-bills.js:71-77`) uses.
- [ ] The bill itself is untouched: `name`, `amount`, `day`, `category`,
      `categoryId` and `lastPaidCycle` are unchanged by a dismissal, and the
      bill still exists in Settings → Bills.
- [ ] **Push reminders still fire for a dismissed bill.**
      `supabase/functions/send-bill-reminders/index.ts` is not modified by this
      ticket — see Do not change.
- [ ] An existing install with no `dismissed_cycle` value behaves exactly as
      before: null is "not dismissed", and no bill disappears as a result of
      this change shipping.
- [ ] Two new `STRINGS` keys at most: the Remove `aria-label` and its toast.
- [ ] No other table, mapper, or screen changes.

## UX / design references

- Follow `docs/UX.md`, including "Undo over confirm".
- Match existing: Accounts' three-circle swipe row
  (`settings-accounts.js:20-29`) for geometry and label conventions;
  `deleteBill`'s undo toast (`settings-bills.js:71-77`) for the restore shape.
- Reuse: `manageRowHtml`, `manageSwipeWrapHtml`, `showToast(msg, undoFn)`,
  `billToRow`, `pushRows`, `syncNow`.
- New design primitives required by this ticket: none.
- Mobile (<1024px) behavior: swipe reveals two circles.
- Desktop (>=1024px) behavior: both actions inline in `.row-actions`.

## Implementation guidance

- **Likely files:** a new `supabase/migrations/*.sql`, `src/sync.js`
  (`:72`, `:73-79`), `src/derived.js` (`upcomingBills`, `:316-320`),
  `src/screens/home.js`, `src/i18n.js`, `tests/derived.test.js`.
- **Why a cycle key and not a boolean.** A boolean would need a clearing path of
  its own, which is the exact defect this workstream exists to fix. Storing the
  cycle makes the dismissal self-expiring, matching how `lastPaidCycle` and
  `last_notified_cycle` already work on this table.
- **The mapper is the risky line, not the SQL.** `rowToBill` builds a fresh
  object literal field by field; a field omitted there is silently dropped on
  every pull, and last-write-wins then pushes the loss back. Add it in both
  directions and confirm a round trip, not just a push.
- **Read `docs/SYNC.md`'s last-write-wins and soft-delete rules before
  editing the mappers** rather than inferring them from the two lines you are
  editing.
- **`upcomingBills()` will hold two cycle comparisons after this** — the paid
  one from `WI-030` and this one. They are independent; a bill hidden by either
  is hidden. Do not collapse them into one condition.
- **Relevant tests:** `tests/derived.test.js` already drives `upcomingBills`
  with mocked time (`:38`, `:48`, `:75-78`). `tests/merge.test.js` and
  `tests/restore.test.js` are the reference for round-tripping a mapped field.
- **Do not change:** `supabase/functions/send-bill-reminders/index.ts` — it
  hand-duplicates `nextBillDueDate`/`daysUntilBillDue`/`billDueCycle`
  (`:19-47`) and selects its own reminder set. Teaching it about
  `dismissed_cycle` is deliberately a separate, separately-verified change (see
  the spec's decision 9). Also do not change those three functions in
  `derived.js`, for the same desync reason.
- **Do not change:** `markBillPaid()`, `unmarkBillPaid()`, or the paid-state
  presentation from `WI-030`/`WI-031`.
- **Escalate back to Claude if:**
  - the migration cannot be written as an additive, nullable,
    `if not exists` column;
  - RLS on `bills` needs any change to allow writing the new column;
  - hiding a dismissed bill turns out to require touching the three ported date
    functions;
  - the dismissal appears to need to affect push reminders to be coherent.

## Out of scope

- Teaching push reminders about `dismissedCycle` (spec decision 9).
- A restore path in Settings for dismissed bills — they return next cycle on
  their own.
- Adding a `billId` to transactions.
- Any change to the reminder algorithm or the three ported date functions.
- Editing or deleting a bill from Home.
